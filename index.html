Storyteller privacy

This page is for the Storyteller app on Google Play. The package name is app.storyteller.nights.

The app does not make you create an account.

You type your own Gemini API key. That key stays on your phone. We do not receive it.

The stories stay on your phone. We do not receive them.

When you ask for a story, the words you type are sent to Google Gemini using your key, so Gemini can write the story. That send is between your phone and Google.

The app does not show ads. We do not sell your stories or your key.
